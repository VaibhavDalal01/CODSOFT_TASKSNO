What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers attempt to trick people into providing sensitive information or visiting fraudulent websites.

Remember: Always be careful with unexpected emails, messages, links, and login pages.

How to Identify Suspicious Emails and Websites

  • Unexpected messages asking for personal information.
  • Links that lead to unfamiliar or suspicious websites.
  • Fake login pages designed to look like legitimate websites.
  • Messages creating pressure or urgency.
  • Requests for passwords or other sensitive information.

Fake Login Pages

A fraudulent login page may be designed to look like a legitimate website. Users should carefully check the website before entering account information.

Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating people into taking an action or revealing information.

Common Techniques

  • Creating a sense of urgency.
  • Pretending to be a trusted person or organization.
  • Using convincing messages to gain trust.
  • Encouraging users to click suspicious links.

Practical Security Tips

  • Think carefully before clicking links in unexpected messages.
  • Check websites carefully before entering information.
  • Do not provide sensitive information to suspicious requests.
  • Be cautious of unexpected emails and messages.
  • Learn to recognize common social engineering techniques.

Case Study

Example: Suspicious Account Message

Imagine receiving a message claiming that your account has a problem and asking you to click a link to log in.

Instead of immediately clicking the link, stop and examine the message carefully. Check whether the request is expected and whether the website appears legitimate.

This simple pause can help prevent users from falling for fraudulent websites and social engineering attempts.
