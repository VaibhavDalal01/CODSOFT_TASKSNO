🛡️ Phishing Awareness Program

Learn how to identify and avoid phishing attacks

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals use fake messages, websites, or emails to trick people into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.

⚠️ Remember: Never share passwords, OTPs, banking details, or other sensitive information through suspicious links or messages.

📧 How to Identify Suspicious Emails

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Be suspicious of unexpected attachments.
  • Watch for urgent or threatening language.
  • Look for spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Verify unusual requests through another trusted method.

🔐 Fake Login Pages

Attackers may create websites that look similar to legitimate login pages. Their goal is to trick users into entering usernames and passwords.

Safety Tip: Check the website address before entering your credentials. Look carefully for unusual domains, spelling changes, or suspicious URLs.

🌐 Fraudulent Websites

  • Check the website domain carefully.
  • Be cautious about unexpected offers or prizes.
  • Look for secure HTTPS connections.
  • Avoid websites reached through suspicious messages.
  • Use official websites or trusted bookmarks when possible.

🎭 Common Social Engineering Techniques

Cybercriminals may manipulate people using techniques such as:

  • Urgency: "Act immediately or your account will be closed."
  • Fear: Creating panic so the victim acts without thinking.
  • Impersonation: Pretending to be a bank, company, teacher, or other trusted person.
  • Curiosity: Using an interesting message to encourage a click.
  • Rewards: Offering fake prizes, discounts, or gifts.

🛡️ Practical Security Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep your operating system and applications updated.
  • Think before clicking links or opening attachments.
  • Verify unexpected requests for sensitive information.
  • Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.

📚 Case Study

A user receives an email claiming to be from a bank. The message says there is a problem with the user's account and asks them to click a link immediately to verify their information.

The email creates urgency and directs the user to a suspicious website. These are common warning signs of a phishing attempt.

What should the user do? Do not click the link. Instead, contact the bank using its official website or official contact information.
